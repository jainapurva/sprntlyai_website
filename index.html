FAQ

Questions we get asked.

How fast can I get value?
Five minutes to set up. Connect your tools, pick your core metric, and Sprntly starts surfacing findings the same day. Your first Monday brief lands the following week.
Will this replace my product manager?
No. Sprntly handles roughly 80% of the analysis, strategy drafting, and roadmap work. Your PM gets that time back for the strategic, cross-functional, and judgment-heavy work that only a human can do.
How is this different from analytics tools I already use?
Analytics tells you what happened. Sprntly tells you why, what to do about it, and prepares the work to ship. Example: your dashboards might show declining activation. Sprntly will tell you that users who customize a dashboard in their first hour retain 4× longer — and write the strategy to make that happen for everyone.
How do we know the findings are accurate?
Every finding ships with the evidence trail it was based on and a confidence score calculated in code (not by the model). If there's no evidence, there's no finding.
Is my data secure?
Yes. SOC 2-certified infrastructure with row-level security. We work with metadata and aggregates only — never raw PII. We don't train on your data.
Where is my data stored?
In your region by default — US (us-east), EU (eu-west), or APAC. Enterprise plans include single-tenant deployments with full data residency control.
